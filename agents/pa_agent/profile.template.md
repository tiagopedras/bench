# Profile

Who the list belongs to, for the PA. Copy this file to `data/profile.md` in your
Bench checkout and fill it in. `data/` is git-ignored, so this stays on your
machine. Wherever it disagrees with `agents/pa_agent/PA.md`, this file wins.

Keep it short. Every `pa-*` skill reads it at the start of every session.

Name: <your name, spelled the way `[to:: ]` and people.md write it>

The board reads that one line too: the review steps of a task handed to an
agent are assigned to this name, and it is how the board knows a card is your
move.

## Who I am

What I do, and what the lists are for. For example: which lists are work and
which are personal, and what kind of work lands in each.

## How I work

What the PA should weigh my tasks against. For example:

- How far my own estimates can be trusted, and whether to pad them.
- What I hand off once it is scoped, and what I always keep for myself.
- Whether logging a problem means "build this" or "remember this".

## How to talk to me

- Language and spelling (for example British or American English).
- How short replies should be.
- Words, punctuation or styles to avoid.
